Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-07. This policy covers the website helloekg.com and the helloEKG email list.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Cardiomind sp. z o.o., Poland (“helloEKG”, “we”). Contact for anything related to your data: hello@helloekg.com.

2. What we collect and why

  • Email list. When you sign up we store your email address, the date and time of signup and of confirmation, where on the page you signed up (for example “case-01”), and — if you finished the ECG Challenge — your score. We use it to send you the weekly ECG case and news about helloEKG, including early access to the English edition. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Abuse prevention. To limit automated signups we keep a one-way hash of your IP address for a short time. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard technical logs (IP address, time, requested URL, browser) to keep the site secure and running. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

The ECG Challenge runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you choose to share your score when signing up.

3. Double opt-in

After you sign up we send a single confirmation email. You are added to the list only after you click the link in it. Unconfirmed addresses are deleted after 30 days.

4. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not load third-party trackers. Fonts are served from our own server.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We use processors that act on our instructions: our hosting provider (home.pl, Poland) and, if we use one to send newsletters, an email delivery provider bound by a data processing agreement. If a provider is located outside the European Economic Area, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent. After that we keep only a record that you unsubscribed, so we do not email you again.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to processing, receive a copy of your data, and withdraw consent at any time — every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or the authority in your country of residence.

8. Medical disclaimer

helloEKG provides educational content for healthcare professionals and students. It is not medical advice and does not replace clinical judgment or local protocols.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell subscribers by email.
